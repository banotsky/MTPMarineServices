MTP Marine Services

Integrated Marine Engineering, Fleet Operations, and ERP-Driven Project Management

1. Corporate Overview

Located in Brgy. New Danglayan, Bauan, Batangas, MTP Marine Services delivers engineering, fabrication, marine repair, and fleet-managed project execution supported by an integrated ERP system.

2. Mission & Vision

To provide reliable marine and industrial services powered by real-time financial monitoring, fleet automation, and data-driven decisions.

3. Core Services

Ship Repair • Mechanical Works • Fabrication • Industrial Projects • Fleet Logistics • Supplier Coordination

4. Project Management System

Full budget allocation, expense tracking per invoice, CEO/Manager oversight, and real-time project profitability monitoring.

5. Financial Monitoring

Invoice-based expense recording, tax/non-tax classification, quantity/unit price tracking, and automatic budget summaries.

6. Fleet Management Division

GPS live tracking, geofence-based auto charging, after-hours driver charging, project/supplier classification, and audit logging.

7. Bidding & Cost Estimation

Historical usage analytics, material consumption forecasting, labor tracking, and profitability simulation for future tenders.

8. Role-Based ERP Access

CEO Dashboard • Manager Controls • Expediter Expense Control • Controlled editing with full audit trail.

9. Automation & Controls

Auto budget computation, supplier visit tagging, working hours rule (7AM–6PM), emergency override approval workflow.

10. Data & Cloud Infrastructure

Supabase-powered cloud database integration with secure login, real-time synchronization, and scalable storage architecture.
